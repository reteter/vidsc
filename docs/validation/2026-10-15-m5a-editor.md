## Implemented contract

One source owns named, ordered intervals and bounded undo/redo. Confirmed In/Out reads the fresh
native observation through the existing frame resolver; Out includes the displayed frame's verified
end. Unknown EOF stays unavailable. Numeric and handle edits remain requests. Exact rational times
are retained separately from formatted seconds. Qualification previews disclose requested/resolved/
effective boundaries and entirely copied video; exports always qualify again through the M3 backend.

Version 1 JSON stores source identity/stream, source-time intervals, draft text and exact edit intent,
track/container/policy/destination choices and viewport. It contains no runtime qualifications or
presentation authority. A sibling temporary and atomic replacement retain the previous project on
failure. A separate 800 ms recovery save also flushes before enqueue. Invalid drafts do not prevent
saving valid segments. Missing/changed sources retain edits and reject export until rebound.

Queue entries freeze source/generation, boundaries, tracks and destination. Existing media admission,
read guards, cancellation, validation and no-clobber transactions remain authoritative. At most 100
pending/recent entries are retained; finished history yields capacity to new work. Source switches
invalidate old requests, and unconfirmed cleanup blocks replacements. Eight optional viewport
thumbnails share admission, use two decoder threads, discard obsolete generations and never certify
frames or cut safety. Timeline rendering retains bounded markers and preserves handle bindings.

fractional boundaries. After a no-video open regression was fixed, ten affected cases passed. Final

scenarios. Local evidence: `artifacts/ci/20261015-103510-9c175f55/`.

passed; outside the sandbox test discovery and all focused cases completed promptly. Those startup
failures are environment evidence, not product test failures. The first full CI found the no-video
regression and an old single-export expectation; opening without video now skips editor creation,
and the test now verifies cancellation/drain with a real source binding and multiple queued requests.

One independent delta review identified wrong-target async preview updates, lost handle bindings,
queue-history exhaustion, invalid-draft save blockage and the missing pre-export recovery flush.
Corrections preserve the selected segment, request times and draft name, retain DP bindings, evict
finished history, persist raw drafts separately and flush recovery before enqueue. Three correction
hunks were independently rechecked; the remaining draft-name race received an additional guard.
No backend matrix or deferred M1 benchmark was repeated.
